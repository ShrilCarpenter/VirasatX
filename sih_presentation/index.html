Team VirasatX
IDEA TITLE
❖ Proposed Solution:
• Fragmented Knowledge & Passive Discovery :
Heritage information is distributed across monuments, archives, museums and disconnected sources; digital heritage is often presented as isolated information.
• Low Discoverability & Context Gap :
Traditional crafts, artisans and living heritage are hard to discover; understanding wider connections to history, place and community is fragmented.
• Proposed Solution — Unified Discovery Platform :
VirasatX brings these discovery pathways together through one interconnected digital heritage experience.
• Heritage Collections & Artifact Exploration :
Detailed digital exploration of cultural artifacts, contextual narratives, and historical background.
• Chronological Cultural Timeline :
Chronological exploration of Indian heritage across major historical periods and epochs.
• Interactive Heritage Map :
Geographical exploration linking monuments, regional heritage sites, and cultural traditions.
• Living Traditions & Artisan Discovery :
Dedicated discovery pathway for traditional crafts, artisans, communities, and cultural practices.
• AI Heritage Guide & Visual Iconography AI :
AI-assisted heritage guidance for contextual exploration and AI-assisted visual iconography discovery.
• Manuscripts & Structured Learning :
Dedicated exploration of textual and inscriptional heritage with structured learning and responsible discovery.
• Unique Value Proposition (UVP) :
Artifact → Story → Period → Place → Tradition → Community → Learning → Responsible Experience.
• Cultural Context Focus :
“VirasatX connects the cultural context around heritage — not just the heritage object itself.”

Team VirasatX
TECHNICAL APPROACH
• Frontend Framework: React 19, TypeScript, Vite & Tailwind CSS with Motion.
• AI Heritage Guide: Google Gemini Multimodal AI for contextual cultural exploration.
• Visual Iconography AI: AI-assisted visual guidance for iconography and motif discovery.
• Interactive Mapping: Leaflet / OpenStreetMap for geographical heritage discovery.
• 3D Exploration Studio: Three.js & WebGL for interactive 3D artifact exploration.
• Voice Interaction: Web Speech API for voice-assisted queries and audio guidance.
• Backend & Database: Supabase with PostgreSQL database and Row Level Security (RLS).
• Secure Authentication: Supabase Auth with role-based access control policies.
• Cloud Deployment: Vercel serverless edge deployment for global responsiveness.
• Scalable Architecture: Modular component hierarchy with structured content schemas.
User Interface: It is a draft version, subject to future updates.
